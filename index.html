02 / MY STORY

Made with patience.
Driven by possibility.

I'm Augistina Mawulawe Azaglo, a 25-year-old dressmaker with a love for clothes that make people feel like themselves.

Since completing senior high school in 2019, I have built my experience through practical sewing. Working for clients and within institutions has taught me that a beautiful garment begins with understanding the person who will wear it.

My first project was a simple skirt. Getting its fit right sparked a curiosity that has stayed with me through every new seam, fabric, and fitting.
